How the vibe score works

An HOA isn't simply good or bad. Fenceline reads two different things and keeps them separate:

Strictness — how much the rulebook governs your day-to-day: rule count, the size of typical fines, and how little discretion the covenants leave you.

Toxicity — how the board treats people: response times, selective enforcement, and the tone residents describe in their reviews.

The single swatch — Chill → Mellow → Strict → Tense → Toxic — is a blend of both, so a strict-but-fair board lands lower than a strict-and-cruel one. The detail page always shows the two axes on their own so you can judge for yourself.

This is a prototype. Scores are illustrative and built from fictional demo reviews — not real associations.
